Features

Built for the work that used to take all day

Every feature is designed around one principle: automate the grunt work, keep a human checkpoint on every consequential decision.

◆

Inbound triage

Every document and message is read, classified, and routed to the correct workflow automatically — no manual sorting.

◆

Human checkpoints

Before any decision ships, a teammate reviews and signs off. Zero autonomous risk, full audit trail.

◆

Always-on coordination

Meridian follows up across email and chat so nothing waits on an unread inbox to move forward.

  2. 02
